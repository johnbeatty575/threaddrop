Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

ThreadDrop ("we," "us," or "our") operates the ThreadDrop social media automation application. This page explains how we handle information when you interact with our content on platforms including TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and Pinterest.

1. Information We Collect

ThreadDrop is a personal automation tool. We do not collect, store, or process personal information from viewers or followers. The application operates solely on behalf of the ThreadDrop account owner to publish content automatically.

2. How We Use Platform APIs

ThreadDrop uses the following platform APIs exclusively to publish content:

  • TikTok Content Posting API — used to upload short-form videos to the ThreadDrop TikTok account
  • YouTube Data API v3 — used to upload videos to the ThreadDrop YouTube channel
  • Instagram Graph API — used to publish Reels to the ThreadDrop Instagram account
  • Pinterest API — used to publish pins to the ThreadDrop Pinterest board

No user data, viewer data, or third-party account data is accessed or stored.

3. Data Storage

All automation runs locally on a private device. No data is transmitted to or stored on any third-party server outside of the platforms listed above.

4. Third-Party Platforms

Content published through ThreadDrop is subject to the privacy policies of each respective platform. We encourage you to review those policies directly.

5. Children's Privacy

ThreadDrop does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Our content is general-interest lifestyle and product content suitable for all ages.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
